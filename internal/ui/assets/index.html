A lease ends when the job says so, when a deadline the user wrote down elapses, or when a human takes it back. Nothing on this page ends one on its own. A ◐ suspect lease means the control plane has not heard a heartbeat from the holder — it does not mean the device is broken, the device has not been released, and a heartbeat that arrives later heals the lease at the same fence.

Steps of the selected job

Which step a job is on, and what it printed. Pick a job above; the rows come from GET /api/v1/jobs/{id}/steps, which is the same view ctl job steps prints. A step that failed carries the exit code, the runner's message and the log it produced, so the answer to “which step failed and why” needs neither a terminal nor a database session.

The ladder

Recovery climbs from the cheapest rung upward and acts on behalf of the holder: the lease keeps its device, the lease clock keeps ticking and the fence never moves. A rung whose blast radius exceeds the live lease's disruption policy is refused, not quietly downgraded.
